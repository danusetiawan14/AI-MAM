# AI-MAM

## AI-Powered Content Intelligence Platform

AI-MAM is an AI-first Content Intelligence Platform designed for broadcasters, OTT platforms, production houses, and content creators.

Unlike traditional Media Asset Management (MAM) systems, AI-MAM automatically understands video content using Artificial Intelligence and transforms media assets into searchable knowledge.

## Vision

Upload Video → AI Understands Content → Instantly Search, Reuse, and Monetize
