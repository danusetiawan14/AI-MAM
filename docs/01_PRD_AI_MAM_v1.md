# AI-MAM Product Requirement Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

Status: Draft

## Vision

AI-Powered Content Intelligence Platform for Broadcasters, OTT Platforms and Content Creators.

## Product Positioning

AI-MAM is an AI-first Content Intelligence Platform that automatically understands video content and transforms media assets into searchable knowledge.

## MVP Scope

- Asset Upload
- AI Transcription
- Auto Tagging
- Keyword Extraction
- Scene Detection
- Search
- Asset Preview
